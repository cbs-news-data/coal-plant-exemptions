Where the EPA granted power plant exemptions

The map below displays the locations of power plant facilities that received exemptions from the EPA along with Census Tracts designated as "disadvantaged" by the Climate and Economic Justice Screening Tool. Nearly 65% of the 68 power plants granted exemptions are located within three miles of these low-income, minority communities.
